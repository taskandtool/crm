# The voice

`brand/voice.md` is the file every writing task reads first: website pages,
ads, posts, emails. Every line quotes the owner's own sentence as evidence
(a guide built on assertions drifts); a line with no sample stays "to fill".

## What to collect

Rank samples by how unpolished they are. Best first:

1. The owner talking or writing to someone: chat, emails, call transcripts,
   replies to reviews.
2. Their own social posts.
3. Their website and brochures, which an agency may have written.

Customers' reviews are not the business's voice, but their phrases go in the
lexicon: they are how buyers describe the problem.

## What to write

- **The card**: reader, speaker, relationship (peer, guide, operator,
  expert, host), register, cadence, vocabulary, proof style (demonstration,
  numbers, examples, process, credentials), humour, taboos, the primary
  action.
- **Three sentences** the business could say, written once the card is
  filled. Show them to the owner: "Does this sound like you?"
- **Signatures**: three to five recognisable moves, each with the sentence
  that shows it and its source.
- **Never**: words and moves the business does not use, with the reason.
- **Rewrites**: three to five pairs, the generic version and the same thing
  in this voice.
- **Lexicon**: protected terms (their names for things, spelt their way),
  customer phrases (verbatim), words the owner uses that a marketer would not.
- **How the sentences run**, measured on the samples: average length,
  shortest, longest, how they open, contractions, exclamation marks.
- **Tone by surface**: what changes and what never changes on a web page,
  an ad, a social post, an email.
- **Sources**: the raw files the samples came from.
