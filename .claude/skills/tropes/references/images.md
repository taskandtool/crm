# Picture tells

Hands and legible text are mostly fixed in current models; what gives a picture away now is the look: light with no source, skin with no pores, no grain, symmetry, a subject cut out against blur. The model's average is a stock photo. Look at full size and at 25%.

| Trope | Fix |
|---|---|
| Waxy, poreless skin; identical bright teeth; matching catchlights in both eyes | Prefer the owner's photographs. When generating, prompt camera behaviour (lens, available light, one source) not quality adjectives |
| Light with no source: everything lit, no shadows, HDR | One dominant light with falloff and real shadow |
| No sensor noise; perfectly clean or uniform grain | Accept grain that deepens in shadow; never "8k, ultra-detailed, cinematic, trending" in the prompt |
| Over-bokeh: sharp cutout subject on a creamy blur, no lens fringing | Deep focus or plausible depth of field for the lens; a subject in a real place |
| Teal-orange or purple-teal grading, oversaturation, edge glow, lens flare | The brand's palette bound to objects; neutral grade |
| Symmetry and centring by default; subject floating on a gradient; rooms with no wear | Off-centre composition, a real setting, signs of use |
| Stock-diverse-team poses: handshakes, high-fives, pointing at a laptop, headset smile, woman laughing at salad | Real staff and customers (with consent) doing the actual work, or no people |
| Glossy 3D icons, clay blobs, isometric gradients, sparkle ✨ as "AI" | Flat or line icons from the design system, or none |
| Garbled, misspelt or extra text; invented logos, numbers, UI or charts in the picture | Words in the picture only when the prompt quotes them; check every letter against the copy; any other lettering means a re-prompt |
| Remaining anatomy errors: complex hand grips, tools held wrong, jewellery melting into skin, hair fusing to the background, broken straight lines behind objects | Inspect at 100%; re-roll; avoid hands-on-tool close-ups unless photographed |
| Generic motifs (lighthouse, cathedral, skyline, abstract waves) unrelated to the business | Picture what this business actually does, in a place it actually serves |
| Generated faces in testimonials | Never. A real photo with consent, or no photo |
